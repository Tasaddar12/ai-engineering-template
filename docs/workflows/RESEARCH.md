# Research and decision workflow

Define one question and the decision it supports. Note facts that may have changed and prefer current primary sources. Record search date, direct links, authority, relevant evidence, conflicting accounts, uncertainty, and any inference.

Use a clean copy of `.ai/templates/RESEARCH.md` for the research record and `.ai/templates/DECISION.md` for a resulting decision. Store filled research under `.ai/research/` and decisions under `.ai/decisions/`; never edit the master templates. Keep quotations short and attributable.

Research informs requirements and decisions but does not grant credentials, provider availability, permissions, or approval. Recheck time-sensitive findings before a later implementation or delivery depends on them.
