# ADR-NNN: Decision

## Context

The constraints and evidence that made this a consequential choice.

## Decision

The actual decision and its human authorization or delegated basis. Proposed
reasoning is not an accepted commitment.

## Alternatives

Plausible alternatives and why they were not selected.

## Consequences

Benefits, costs, risks and conditions that should trigger reconsideration.

## Related phase and behavior

Relevant phase and SPEC references. This document owns rationale, not a second
copy of the behavioral contract.

When replacing an accepted decision, create a new ADR referencing it and set the
old decision's status to superseded with a link. Preserve historical reasoning.
