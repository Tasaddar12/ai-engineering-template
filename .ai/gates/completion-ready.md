# Completion ready

## Transition

Before moving a PLAN or FIX to done.

## PASS criteria

- Current contracts and approved outcomes are verified with no unresolved
  required checks.
- The PLAN's Contract changes are delivered or explicitly reconciled within the
  approved outcome; a FIX has real before/after proof.
- Required independent review is complete and user acceptance is evidenced.
- The delivery the user authorized is complete and observed; a pushed draft
  alone is not acceptance.
- Current records and links agree, and remaining unrelated work has an owning
  record.

## Evidence

Link verification, review, user acceptance and Git/hosting observations. Do not
use a checked task list as a substitute for working behavior.

## On FAIL

Keep the result in review or FIX open and report what prevents closure.
